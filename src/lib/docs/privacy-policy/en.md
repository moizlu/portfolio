# Privacy Policy

Moizlu (hereinafter referred to as "I") establishes the following Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as "this Policy") regarding the handling of users' personal information on this website (hereinafter referred to as "this Site").

## 1. Handling of Personal Information

This Site collects and uses the following information through the contact form:

1. Name, email address, subject, and message entered in the form
2. IP address
    - Used solely for spam detection and security purposes. It is stored only for the necessary period after being hashed, and cannot be viewed by external services other than the servers required for processing, nor by us in a usable form.

In addition, for the purpose of understanding usage of this Site and preventing unauthorized access, the following information may be collected by external services:

1. Statistical information (non-personally identifiable information)
2. Information related to CAPTCHA authentication (IP address, browser information, mouse movements, etc.)
    - This is information related to authentication using Cloudflare Turnstile. Collected information is processed appropriately in accordance with Cloudflare's Privacy Policy and will not be used for purposes other than those intended, such as advertising retargeting.
    - I cannot view information collected for this purpose.

## 2. Purpose of Use of Personal Information

Collected personal information will be used only for the following purposes:

- To improve services by analyzing site usage (statistical information only)
- To respond to inquiries and verify identity
- To prevent spam and unauthorized access

## 3. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

This Site will not disclose personal information obtained to third parties without the user's consent, except as required by law.

## 5. Spam Prevention and Security

This Site implements the following measures for safe operation:

- CAPTCHA (Cloudflare Turnstile)
    - Used to detect and block fraudulent operations by bots.
    - For authentication, various information may be collected within the scope of Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
- Rate Limiting (Upstash)
    - To prevent spam attacks that abuse this Site, users' IP addresses and entered email addresses are converted into an irreversible form and used for temporary limits on the number of submissions. This Site does not store or use raw IP addresses, email addresses, or other information for this purpose.

## 6. Security Measures

This Site implements the following technical security measures:

- SSL/TLS encryption
    - All communications are encrypted.
- Hashing of identifying information
    - Identifying information used for rate limiting (IP addresses, email addresses) is converted into a form that is extremely difficult to restore through hashing, and is stored only for the period necessary for processing.

## 7. Procedures for Requests Regarding Retained Personal Data

Users may request disclosure, correction, addition or deletion, suspension of use, or erasure of their personal data retained by this Site.

If a request is made, I will verify the user's identity and respond promptly.
Please contact us via the contact form or email address below.

## 8. Disclaimer

I assume no responsibility whatsoever for the information, services, etc. provided on other sites accessed via links from this Site.

## 9. Changes to and Application of this Policy

This Policy may be changed without prior notice to the extent that it does not violate laws and regulations. In such cases, the change will take effect immediately upon being displayed on this website.
